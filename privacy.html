Privacy Policy

Application: Shopping List (shop.chebur.shopping_list.twa)
Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Shopping List application (accessible via Google Play and https://shopping-list.chebur.shop) handles user information. Your privacy is important to us, and the application is designed to work without collecting personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Shopping List does not collect, store on external servers, sell, or share any personally identifiable information.

  • All shopping lists, items, categories, and budget settings you create are stored locally on your device (using your browser/app local storage).
  • No user account, registration, or login is required to use the application.
  • We do not track your location, contacts, photos, or browsing history.

2. Third-Party Services

The application is distributed through Google Play. When you purchase or download the application, Google Play may process payment and technical diagnostic data in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your payment details.

The application does not contain third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all app data is stored exclusively on your device, you have full control over it at all times. You can delete your data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

4. Children's Privacy

Shopping List does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or adults. The application is safe for general audiences.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
